Hikma
Education · Android

Malayalam quizzes for curious minds

Hikma is a Malayalam quiz app for students, quiz lovers and lifelong learners. Practise general knowledge, Kerala, science, literature, cinema, the Quran and more until you master every question, then take an exam and see how you rank against other learners.

Features

Learn it, master it, prove it

Practice that adapts to what you miss, exams that show your progress, and leaderboards that keep it fun.

📚

Topics in Malayalam

  • General knowledge: GK, current affairs, special days and Kerala
  • Language & arts: Malayalam, literature and cinema
  • Science: science and space
  • Islamic studies: the Quran surah by surah, and the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
  • Exam-style sets: talent searches, class levels, English and mental ability
🎯

Practise until you master it

  • Mastery practice: missed questions come back until you get them right
  • Recall checks: correct answers return later to make sure they stick
  • Continue learning: progress is saved; jump back in from the home screen
📝

Exams & review

  • Choose your length: quick 10, standard 20 or full 30 questions
  • Flag and check: mark tricky questions and see a summary before you submit
  • Instant results: score, accuracy and time the moment you finish, ready to share as an image
  • Answer review: see every answer, or filter to just the ones you missed
  • Practise my mistakes: turn wrong answers into a focused drill
🏆

Leaderboards

  • Fair ranking: ranked by best score, with the faster time winning ties
  • Weekly board: resets every Monday, so everyone gets a fresh chance
  • Podium & your rank: top three on the podium, and your rank even outside the top 10
✨

Made for learning

  • Clean design: a calm, distraction-free interface
  • Haptic feedback: feel every answer, or switch it off in Settings
  • Share results: send your score to friends
🔐

Your account

  • Google Sign-In: get started in one tap
  • Email & password: with password reset if you are ever locked out
  • Delete anytime: remove your account and data from inside the app

Privacy

Safe for learners of all ages

How Hikma handles your data

Hikma uses your email address to sign you in, stores your quiz activity to save progress and power the leaderboard (other learners see your first name and best score), and uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob as described in full in our privacy policy. You can delete your account and data at any time from your profile in the app.

Suitable for children

All ads are child-directed, non-personalized and general-audience, and the app does not use the advertising ID.

Built with React Native and the Hermes engine. Firebase powers authentication, data storage, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration and push notifications.
