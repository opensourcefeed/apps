Learn Something New, One Quiz at a Time

Hikma is a multiple-choice quiz app that helps you test and build your knowledge across a range of categories. Browse topics, answer questions at your own pace, review exactly what you got right or wrong, and see how you rank against other learners on the leaderboard.

v3.0 Pre-release
📱 Coming Soon to Google Play

✨ Key Features

🗂️

Category Browser

  • Organized Topics: Browse categories and sub-categories to find quizzes that interest you
  • Mixed Quiz: Take a comprehensive quiz across a whole category tree
❓

MCQ Quizzes

  • Multiple Choice: Answer questions at your own pace
  • Question Review: See a visual overview of answered and skipped questions before finishing
  • Instant Feedback: Review correct and incorrect answers after every quiz
🏆

Leaderboard

  • Rankings: See how you stack up against other learners per category
  • Score Tracking: Total points, quizzes taken, average and best score
🔐

Sign-In Options

  • Google Sign-In: Get started quickly with your Google account
  • Email & Password: Traditional sign-up and login
  • Password Reset: Recover access if you're ever locked out

🔒 Privacy

Hikma collects your email address for sign-in, your quiz activity to power scores and the leaderboard, and uses Google Analytics and AdMob as described in full in our Privacy Policy.

Technical Stack: Built with React Native & TypeScript. Uses Firebase for authentication, data storage, analytics and push notifications, and the Hermes JavaScript engine for performance.
