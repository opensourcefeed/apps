Malayalam Quizzes for Curious Minds

Hikma is a Malayalam quiz app for students, quiz lovers and lifelong learners. Practise general knowledge, Kerala, science, literature, cinema, the Quran and more until you master every question, then take an exam and see how you rank against other learners.

✨ Key Features

📚

Topics in Malayalam

  • General Knowledge: GK, current affairs, special days and Kerala
  • Language & Arts: Malayalam, literature and cinema
  • Science: Science and space
  • Islamic Studies: Quran surah by surah, and the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
  • Exam-Style Sets: Talent searches, class levels, English and mental ability
🎯

Practise Until You Master It

  • Mastery Practice: Missed questions come back until you get them right
  • Recall Checks: Correct answers return later to make sure they stick
  • Continue Learning: Your progress is saved; jump back in from the home screen
📝

Exams & Review

  • Choose Your Length: Quick 10, standard 20 or full 30 questions
  • Instant Results: Score, accuracy and time the moment you finish
  • Answer Review: See every answer, or filter to just the ones you missed
  • Practice My Mistakes: Turn wrong answers into a focused drill
🏆

Leaderboards

  • Fair Ranking: Ranked by your best score, with the faster time winning ties
  • Weekly Board: Resets every Monday, so everyone gets a fresh chance
  • Podium & Your Rank: Top three on the podium, and your own rank even outside the top 10
✨

Made for Learning

  • Clean Design: A calm, distraction-free interface
  • Haptic Feedback: Feel every answer, or switch it off in Settings
  • Share Results: Send your score to friends
🔐

Your Account

  • Google Sign-In: Get started in one tap
  • Email & Password: With password reset if you're ever locked out
  • Delete Anytime: Remove your account and data from inside the app

🔒 Privacy

Hikma uses your email address to sign you in, stores your quiz activity to save progress and power the leaderboard (other learners see your first name and best score), and uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob as described in full in our Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and data at any time from the app's menu.

Technical Stack: Built with React Native. Uses Firebase for authentication, data storage, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration and push notifications, and the Hermes JavaScript engine for performance.
