Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Hikma is a multiple-choice quiz app, with quizzes mostly in Malayalam. This policy explains what information Hikma collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up or sign in, we collect your email address and, if you choose email/password registration, your name and gender. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Quiz activity: Your quiz answers, scores, quiz duration, practice progress, and leaderboard records (your best score and its time, all-time and for the current week) are stored so you can track progress and compete with other learners. Your first name and best score are visible to other learners on the leaderboard.
  • Data stored on your device: Practice progress and app settings (such as haptic feedback and ad frequency counters) are also kept in local storage on your device so you can resume where you left off.
  • Usage analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect app usage data such as screens viewed and general app interactions, to help us understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Advertising identifiers: Hikma displays ads through Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads.
  • Crash reports: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, including device and app details and your account's user ID, so we can find and fix problems.
  • App configuration: Firebase Remote Config uses an app installation identifier to deliver settings, such as how often ads are shown, without an app update.
  • Push notification token: If notifications are enabled, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications to your device, which requires a device-specific messaging token.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To save your quiz progress, scores, and display your ranking on leaderboards.
  • To understand app usage, diagnose crashes, and improve features and performance.
  • To serve advertisements that help keep the app free.
  • To send you push notifications, where applicable.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account and quiz data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore). All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

Hikma uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Firestore, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Review — lets you rate the app without leaving it; your review is handled by Google Play — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob Data Collection

5. Your Choices

  • You can delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app: open the menu and choose Delete account. This removes your sign-in account, your profile, your exam records and leaderboard entries, and your practice progress, both on our servers and on your device. You can also request deletion by contacting us at the email below.
  • You can reset or change your password at any time from within the app.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings.
  • You can turn notifications and haptic feedback on or off at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

Hikma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to request data deletion, contact us at contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com.
