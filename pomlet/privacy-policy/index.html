Pomlet Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · previous version July 21, 2026

Pomlet is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Pomlet app. In short: everything stays on your phone, and we never receive any of it.

1. Data Collection

  • Personal Data: Pomlet does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our or anyone else's servers. There is no account.
  • Usage Data: Your timer sessions, statistics, history, daily goal and settings are stored locally on your device using MMKV storage.
  • No Analytics: Pomlet does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or behavioural tracking.

2. Data Usage

  • Your data is used solely to provide the app's features on your device (timer, statistics, streaks and reminders).
  • No data is shared with us or with third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Shows the running countdown, tells you when a focus or break session ends, and, only if you turn it on, sends an evening streak reminder.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Keeps a timer you started running accurately while the screen is off or another app is open.
  • Schedule Exact Alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Optional. Lets the backup "session complete" alert arrive on time if Android stops the app during a session.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): Haptic feedback and completion vibration. You can turn haptics off in Settings.
  • Pomlet does not request the Internet permission and cannot connect to the internet.

4. Backups and Export

  • Android backup: If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Pomlet's settings, statistics and history in your device backup, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your Google account; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.
  • Export / import: Pomlet can export your data as text (a backup file or a CSV of sessions) through Android's share sheet. Where it goes is entirely your choice. Importing a backup replaces the data on your device and is never uploaded anywhere by Pomlet.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play In-App Review: After a few completed sessions, Pomlet may ask Google Play to show its standard rating card once. Pomlet does not see whether you rated it or what you wrote; any review you leave is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Links in the app (rate, privacy policy, send feedback) open your browser, the Play Store or your email app. Anything you send by email reaches us only because you chose to send it.
  • Pomlet does not use any advertising networks, analytics services or cloud services.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using MMKV, a high-performance key-value storage library.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data from the device (except any copy in your own Android backup or exports).

7. Children's Privacy

Pomlet does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any users. The app is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com.
