Pomlet
Productivity · Android

Focus better, achieve more

Pomlet is a calm, privacy-first Pomodoro timer. Work in focused sessions with short and long breaks, set a daily goal, keep your streak going and see your progress at a glance. All data stays on your device.

  • Android 7.0+
  • Pre-release
  • No ads
  • Fully offline
Coming soon toGoogle Play
 See features

Features

A focus timer that stays out of your way

Everything you need for deep work sessions, and nothing you don't.

⏱️

Focus timer

  • Presets or your own: 25/5, 50/10, 90/20, or any focus length from 1 to 120 minutes
  • Your cycle: a long break every 2 to 8 sessions, shown as dots under the timer
  • Auto-start: optionally start breaks and focus sessions by themselves
  • Calm motion: an animated ring and colours that fade between focus and breaks
🔔

Works in the background

  • Live notification: the countdown with Pause, Skip and Stop buttons
  • Backup alert: you still hear when a session ends, even if Android closes the app
  • Five sounds: bell, chime, wood, gong or beep, with a preview
  • Haptics: gentle vibration feedback, which you can switch off
🔥

Goals & streaks

  • Daily goal: pick how many sessions to aim for and watch today's ring fill
  • Streaks: consecutive days of focus, with one automatic freeze a week
  • Evening reminder: an optional nudge if you haven't focused yet that day
  • Celebrations: when a session ends and when you reach your goal
📊

Statistics & history

  • Totals: pomodoros completed and hours of focus
  • This week: a seven-day chart of your sessions
  • History: every session, grouped by day
  • Crash recovery: a session interrupted by Android is credited when you come back
💾

Your data, your way

  • Export: a full backup, or your sessions as a CSV spreadsheet
  • Import: restore a backup on any phone
  • Android backup: your stats move with you to a new phone
♿

Made for everyone

  • TalkBack: every control is labelled
  • Reduce motion: follows Android's "Remove animations" setting
  • Themes: light, dark, or follow the system

Privacy & security

Your focus data never leaves your phone

Privacy-first architecture

  • 100% local storage: sessions, statistics and settings stay on your device
  • Zero tracking: no analytics SDKs, no behavioural tracking
  • No ads: a clean, distraction-free interface
  • No account required: start focusing immediately
  • No internet permission: the app can't send your data anywhere

Read the full Pomlet privacy policy.

Built for reliability

Pomlet is built with React Native 0.86 and TypeScript, uses MMKV for fast local storage, the Hermes JavaScript engine for performance, and an Android foreground service plus an OS-scheduled backup alert for reliable background timing.
