Our approach

What goes into every app

A few principles we hold ourselves to, from the first sketch to every update.

Privacy by design

We collect only what an app needs. ExpenseWise and Pomora keep everything on your device.

Plain-language policies

Every app has a readable privacy policy that says exactly what is collected and why.

Fast and lightweight

Built with React Native and the Hermes engine, with local storage that keeps apps snappy.

Calm, focused design

Clean interfaces, dark mode and accessibility options, so the app works for you.
