Focus Better, Achieve More

Pomora is a beautiful, privacy-first Pomodoro timer designed to help you stay focused and productive. Use the proven Pomodoro Technique to break your work into focused intervals with short and long breaks. Track your progress with detailed statistics, review your session history, and customize every aspect of your focus routine. All data stays securely on your device.

📱 Coming Soon to Google Play

✨ Key Features

⏱️

Focus Timer

  • Configurable Durations: Set custom focus, short break, and long break intervals
  • Animated Ring: Beautiful circular progress indicator with smooth animations
  • Phase Cycling: Automatic transitions between focus and break sessions
  • Full Controls: Start, pause, resume, reset, and skip with ease
  • Background Timer: Keeps running reliably via Android foreground service
📊

Statistics & Insights

  • Total Pomodoros: Track your lifetime focus sessions
  • Focus Time: See cumulative hours spent in deep work
  • Daily Streak: Build and maintain productive habits
  • Today's Summary: Quick glance at your daily progress
  • Daily History: Browse stats by date with a scrollable list
📝

Session History

  • Complete Log: Every session recorded with timestamp and duration
  • Color-Coded: Visual distinction between focus and break sessions
  • Date Grouping: Sessions organized by Today, Yesterday, and date
  • Crash Recovery: Automatic session restoration if the app is interrupted
⚙️

Customization

  • Duration Steppers: Fine-tune focus and break lengths with +/− controls
  • Auto-Start: Optionally auto-start breaks and focus sessions
  • Sound Alerts: Toggle completion chime on or off
  • Notifications: Toggle completion alerts with vibration
  • Persistent Settings: Your preferences saved instantly via MMKV storage
🔔

Sound & Notifications

  • Completion Chime: Audible beep when a session ends
  • Alert & Vibration: Get notified even when not looking at the screen
  • Respects Settings: Sound and notifications independently toggleable
  • Background Alerts: Notifications delivered via foreground service
🔒

Privacy & Reliability

  • 100% Local Storage: All data stored on-device using MMKV
  • No Internet Required: Works completely offline
  • Zero Tracking: No analytics, no ads, no data collection
  • Session Persistence: Timer state survives app backgrounding and crashes

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our priority. Pomora is built on a privacy-first architecture:

  • 100% Local Storage: All session data, statistics, and settings stay on your device using MMKV storage
  • Zero Tracking: No analytics SDKs, no behavioral tracking, no data mining
  • No Ads: Clean, distraction-free interface without advertisements
  • No Account Required: Start focusing immediately without sign-up
  • Offline First: Works perfectly without internet connection
  • Open Communication: Full transparency about data handling in our Privacy Policy

Technical Stack: Built with React Native 0.86 & TypeScript. Uses MMKV for fast local storage, Hermes JavaScript engine for performance, and Android foreground service for reliable background timing.
