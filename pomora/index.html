Pomora
Productivity · Android

Focus better, achieve more

Pomora is a beautiful, privacy-first Pomodoro timer. Break work into focused intervals with short and long breaks, track your progress with detailed statistics, review your session history and tune every part of your routine. All data stays on your device.

  • Version 0.0.1
  • Pre-release
  • No ads
  • Fully offline
Coming soon toGoogle Play
 See features

Features

A focus timer that stays out of your way

Everything you need for deep work sessions, and nothing you don't.

⏱️

Focus timer

  • Configurable durations: custom focus, short break and long break intervals
  • Animated ring: a circular progress indicator with smooth animations
  • Phase cycling: automatic transitions between focus and breaks
  • Full controls: start, pause, resume, reset and skip
  • Background timer: keeps running reliably via an Android foreground service
📊

Statistics & insights

  • Total pomodoros: your lifetime focus sessions
  • Focus time: cumulative hours spent in deep work
  • Daily streak: build and keep productive habits
  • Today's summary: your daily progress at a glance
  • Daily history: browse stats by date
📝

Session history

  • Complete log: every session recorded with time and duration
  • Colour-coded: focus and break sessions at a glance
  • Date grouping: organised by Today, Yesterday and date
  • Crash recovery: sessions restored automatically if the app is interrupted
⚙️

Customisation

  • Duration steppers: fine-tune lengths with +/− controls
  • Auto-start: optionally start breaks and focus sessions automatically
  • Sound alerts: toggle the completion chime
  • Notifications: toggle completion alerts with vibration
  • Persistent settings: preferences saved instantly
🔔

Sound & notifications

  • Completion chime: an audible cue when a session ends
  • Alert & vibration: notified even when you're not looking
  • Independent toggles: sound and notifications controlled separately
  • Background alerts: delivered via the foreground service
🔒

Privacy & reliability

  • 100% local storage: all data stored on-device
  • No internet required: works completely offline
  • Zero tracking: no analytics, no ads, no data collection
  • Session persistence: timer state survives backgrounding and crashes

Privacy & security

Your focus data never leaves your phone

Privacy-first architecture

  • 100% local storage: sessions, statistics and settings stay on your device
  • Zero tracking: no analytics SDKs, no behavioural tracking
  • No ads: a clean, distraction-free interface
  • No account required: start focusing immediately

Read the full Pomora privacy policy.

Built for reliability

Pomora is built with React Native 0.86 and TypeScript, uses MMKV for fast local storage, the Hermes JavaScript engine for performance, and an Android foreground service for reliable background timing.

React Native 0.86 · TypeScript · MMKV · Hermes
