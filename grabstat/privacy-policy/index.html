GrabStat Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to GrabStat, developed by Tahrik Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our WhatsApp status saver handles your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 WhatsApp Status Files

  • Scoped folder access only: GrabStat requests Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) permission for exactly one folder - WhatsApp's own Statuses folder (Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/.Statuses). It cannot see any other folder, app or file on your device.
  • Read, not uploaded: status photos and videos are read from that folder only to show them in the app and to copy a file you choose to save into your own device gallery. They are never uploaded to our or any third-party server.
  • Saved items: files you save are copied to a "GrabStat" album in your device's gallery, using Android's standard camera roll APIs, so the app can show you what you've already saved.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Android version, device model, and app version for compatibility purposes
  • Usage Data: anonymous app events such as opening the app, granting the folder permission, and saving or sharing a status, collected with Google Firebase Analytics
  • Crash Reports: when the app crashes, a report with the error, device model, Android version and app version, collected with Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID for ad personalization (can be reset in Android settings)

1.3 Information Not Collected

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other personally identifiable information
  • Your WhatsApp messages, contacts or chat content - GrabStat never accesses WhatsApp's chat database
  • Photos, documents or files outside the one Statuses folder
  • Passwords or authentication credentials - there is no account
  • Banking or payment information

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Functionality

  • Display the photos and videos currently in the WhatsApp Statuses folder, split into Images and Videos tabs
  • Save a status you choose to your device gallery, and show it in the app's Saved tab
  • Let you share a status through Android's native share sheet

2.2 Advertising

  • Display banner advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Support the free availability of the app through ad revenue
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via Android settings

2.3 App Improvement

  • Identify and fix bugs or performance issues using crash reports
  • Understand which features (save, share, Saved tab) are used, to guide future updates

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local and On-Device Storage

  • Saved files stay on your device: a save copies the file into your own device gallery; it is not uploaded anywhere
  • No cloud sync: GrabStat does not run its own servers for status files, and does not back them up

3.2 Data Retention

  • Saved files persist in your gallery until you delete them yourself, from the app's Saved tab or your device's gallery app
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase for a limited period (up to 14 months for analytics events and 90 days for crash reports) and then deleted
  • Uninstalling the app removes the folder permission; files you already saved to your gallery are not affected

3.3 Security Measures

  • Folder access uses Android's Storage Access Framework, which is scoped and revocable by the system - not a broad storage permission
  • No transmission of status file contents to our or any third-party server
  • Analytics and crash reports are sent over encrypted connections

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

GrabStat uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • AdMob may use your Advertising ID for personalized ads
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings
  • AdMob may collect device information and usage data

4.2 Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

GrabStat uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find and fix crashes. This data is anonymous: it is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name, email or phone number. It is processed by Google according to the Firebase privacy information.

  • Firebase Analytics: app opens and in-app events listed in section 1.2
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, including the last few in-app events before the crash
  • We use this data only to improve the app. We do not sell it or use it for advertising

4.3 Google Play Services

  • App updates and distribution through Google Play Store
  • Subject to Google Play's Privacy Policy

5. Permissions

GrabStat requests only what it needs to do its one job:

  • Storage Access Framework (one folder): to read the WhatsApp Statuses folder and let you save from it. You grant this through Android's own folder picker, scoped to that folder only, and can revoke it anytime in Android Settings > Apps > GrabStat > Permissions.
  • Internet: to load ads and send anonymous analytics/crash reports.

GrabStat does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or your general photo library.

6. Children's Privacy

GrabStat does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app itself only displays media that is already present in the WhatsApp Statuses folder on the device it runs on.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Data Control

  • Revoke folder access: Android Settings > Apps > GrabStat > Permissions, at any time
  • Delete saved files: remove them from the Saved tab or your device's gallery app
  • Advertising Settings: manage ad personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads

7.2 Opt-Out Options

  • Disable personalized ads through Android device settings
  • Reset your Advertising ID to prevent ad tracking continuity
  • Analytics and crash reports: there is no in-app switch yet. To have this anonymous data deleted, email us; uninstalling the app stops all collection

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Google Play Store policies and guidelines
  • Android app privacy requirements
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles for EU users
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) principles for California users

  • ✅ We don't collect personal information, and status files are never uploaded anywhere

  • ✅ The app shows ads to stay free
  • ✅ You can revoke the folder permission anytime in Android settings
