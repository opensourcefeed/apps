ExpenseWise
Finance · Android

Your money, clearly. Privately.

ExpenseWise is a free, privacy-first expense tracker. Log spending in seconds with smart category suggestions, see patterns in clear charts, set monthly budgets and stay in control. Everything stays on your device, with local backups whenever you want them.

  • Version 1.3.3
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Works offline

Features

Everything you need to master your budget

Fast entry, useful insights and full control over your data, without accounts or subscriptions.

💰

Smart expense management

  • Quick entry: add expenses in seconds with an intuitive interface
  • Smart categories: suggestions based on your spending history
  • Built-in calculator: do the maths while entering amounts
  • Custom categories: create and manage your own expense and income categories
  • Flexible modes: expense-only or full income and expense tracking
📊

Budgets & analytics

  • Monthly budgets: set spending limits and track progress in real time
  • Visual charts: pie and line charts for spending patterns
  • Smart insights: daily averages, top categories and trends
  • Category breakdown: see exactly where your money goes
  • History: compare this month with last month
🔐

Privacy & data control

  • Local-first storage: all records stored on your device with SQLite
  • File backup: export your data to any folder on your device
  • Zero tracking: no analytics, no ads, no data sharing
  • Complete control: import, export or delete your data anytime
🎨

Thoughtful experience

  • Modern design: clean interface with smooth animations
  • Dark mode: easy on the eyes, day or night
  • Calendar view: pick dates easily
  • Swipe actions: edit or delete records with a gesture
  • Offline ready: works perfectly without an internet connection

Privacy & security

Your finances are nobody else's business

Privacy-first architecture

  • 100% local storage: financial data stays on your device in a secure SQLite database
  • Zero tracking: no analytics SDKs, no behavioural tracking, no data mining
  • No ads: a clean, distraction-free interface
  • No account required: start tracking immediately, no sign-up

Read the full ExpenseWise privacy policy.

Built to last

ExpenseWise is built with React Native and Expo Router, stores data in SQLite, runs on the Hermes JavaScript engine for performance, and follows modern Android security best practices.
