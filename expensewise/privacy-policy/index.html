Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2025

ExpenseWise is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the ExpenseWise app.

1. Data Collection

  • Personal Data: ExpenseWise does not collect or store any personal data on our servers.
  • Usage Data: All expense records, categories, and budgets are stored locally on your device.
  • Backups: If you choose, you can back up your data to your own Google Drive account. This is optional and requires your explicit consent.

2. Data Usage

  • Your data is used solely for providing the app’s features on your device.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Storage access is required to save and restore your data backups.
  • Google Drive access is required only if you enable cloud backup.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Drive is used for optional backup and restore. Your data is never sent to our servers.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com.
