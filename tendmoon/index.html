Tendmoon
Health & Fitness · Android

Know your cycle, keep it private

Tendmoon is a calm, private period tracker. See where you are in your cycle, when your next period is likely, and how you have been feeling. There is no account and no cloud: everything stays in an encrypted database on your phone.

  • Android 7.0+
  • Coming soon
  • No ads
  • No internet access

Features

Everything you need to understand your cycle

Honest estimates, quick logging and clear insights, without giving up your privacy.

🌙

Honest predictions

  • Date ranges: your next period and fertile window as a range, not a single guess
  • Confidence level: low, medium or high, based on how regular your history is
  • Learns from you: likely missed logs don't skew your averages
  • Explained: "How is this calculated?" shows the reasoning behind each estimate
📝

Quick daily logging

  • Period days: start, extend or end a period in one tap
  • How you feel: flow, symptoms, mood and notes
  • Fertility signs: cervical mucus and ovulation (LH) tests
  • Optional intimacy log: off by default; record protection alongside your fertile window
  • Add button: log a period or, if you turn it on, intimacy from the Today, Calendar and Insights tabs
📅

Calendar & phases

  • Month view: logged and predicted periods, fertile window and logged days
  • Cycle ring: today's cycle day and phase at a glance
  • Phase guides: plain-language explanations with sources from the NHS and ACOG
📊

Insights

  • Averages: cycle length and period length
  • Regularity: a score and the typical variation in days
  • Cycle chart: your last twelve cycle lengths
  • Patterns: symptoms that tend to appear in each phase
🔔

Discreet reminders

  • When you need them: before your period, if it is late, before the fertile window or ovulation, a daily check-in or a contraception reminder
  • Discreet by default: notifications never mention periods or fertility
  • Scheduled on your phone: no push service, no server
♿

Made for everyone

  • TalkBack: controls and charts are labelled for screen readers
  • Large text: layouts adapt to Android's font size settings
  • Themes: light, dark, or follow the system

Privacy & security

Your cycle data never leaves your phone

Private by design

  • No account, no servers: we never receive your data, so we can't share or sell it
  • No internet permission: the app can't send your data anywhere
  • Encrypted on your phone: SQLCipher database with a key held in the Android Keystore
  • No cloud backup by the system: Android auto-backup is turned off
  • Zero tracking: no analytics, ads or crash-reporting SDKs

Read the full Tendmoon privacy policy.

You stay in control

  • App lock: a PIN or your fingerprint, with lockout after repeated wrong PINs
  • Screenshot blocking: also hides the app in the recent-apps view
  • Encrypted backups: protected with a passphrase you choose, saved wherever you choose
  • Delete everything: one action erases all data from the device

Expo · React Native · TypeScript · SQLCipher

ℹ️ Tendmoon gives estimates for personal awareness. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition and is not a method of contraception.
