Tendmoon Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Tendmoon (Android package org.tahrik.tendmoon) is a period and cycle tracker published by Tahrik Studio. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the app. In short: Tendmoon works entirely on your phone. We do not run servers for the app, there are no user accounts, and we cannot see, collect, sell or share your data.

1. Data Collection

  • We collect nothing. Tendmoon does not send any data to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or ads: the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No internet access: the app does not request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

2. What the App Stores on Your Device

Everything you enter stays in a database on your phone:

  • period dates and cycle settings;
  • daily logs: flow, symptoms, mood, cervical mucus, ovulation (LH) tests and notes;
  • intimacy entries and protection used, only if you turn on this optional feature (it is off by default);
  • reminder times and app settings.

This data is used only to show your calendar, estimates, insights and reminders on your device.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption: the database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256) using a random key generated on your device and kept in the Android Keystore. A copy of the database file cannot be read without that key.
  • App lock: if you set a PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored, in the same secure storage. Repeated wrong PINs lock the app for a growing period.
  • Screenshots: while the app lock or "Block screenshots" is on, Android blocks screenshots and hides the app's content in the recent-apps view.
  • No system cloud backup: Android's automatic backup is turned off for Tendmoon, so the system does not copy your data to Google Drive.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): shows the reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no push service is used.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): restores your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): lets you unlock the app with your fingerprint if you turn on the app lock. Your fingerprint is handled by Android and never reaches the app.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): vibration for reminders.
  • Screen capture detection (DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE): required on Android 14 and later by the component that blocks screenshots. Tendmoon does not record or react to your screenshots, and the permission gives no access to your data.
  • Tendmoon does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

5. Notifications

With "Discreet notifications" on (the default), reminder text never mentions periods or fertility. It reads only "Tendmoon: You have a reminder". Notifications are also marked private, so their content is hidden on a locked screen.

6. Backups and Export

  • Your choice: you can create a backup file in Settings → Backup & restore. Android asks where to save it, for example your phone's storage or a cloud drive. From then on, the privacy policy of the place you chose applies.
  • Encrypted by default: backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose (scrypt and XChaCha20-Poly1305). You can export an unencrypted file only after a warning.
  • Import: restoring a backup reads the file you pick on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • We never receive your backup files.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Tendmoon does not use any third-party services, SDKs that collect data, advertising networks or cloud services.
  • Links in the app (for example to the medical sources behind the phase guides, or to this policy) open in your browser. Only the web address is passed to the browser, never your data.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and portability: export a backup at any time.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete all data erases all records, your PIN and scheduled reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes all data from the device (except backup files you saved elsewhere).
  • Because we hold no personal data, we cannot access, correct or delete data on your behalf, and we have nothing to hand over to anyone, including in response to legal requests.

9. Health Information

Tendmoon provides estimates for personal awareness. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition and is not a method of contraception. Talk to a healthcare professional about anything that worries you.

10. Children's Privacy

Tendmoon is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The optional intimacy log is off by default.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date, and the app will ask you to review important changes before you continue.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com.
