FileMint Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

FileMint (Android package org.tahrik.filemint) is an offline image and file converter published by Tahrik Studio. This policy explains how your files and data are handled when you use the app. In short: FileMint works entirely on your phone. We do not run servers for the app, there are no user accounts, and we cannot see, collect, sell or share your files or data.

1. Data Collection

  • We collect nothing. FileMint does not send any data to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or ads: the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No internet access: the app does not request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet or upload files.

2. How Your Files Are Handled

  • You choose the files: images are opened through Android's system file picker. FileMint only receives access to the files you select.
  • On-device processing: converting, compressing, resizing and creating PDFs happen on your phone using Android's built-in image and PDF libraries.
  • Temporary results: results are kept in the app's private cache until you save or share them. Leftover temporary files are removed the next time the app starts, and you can clear them at any time in Settings → Storage.
  • Saving: when you tap Save, Android asks where to store the file, for example your phone's storage, an SD card or a cloud drive. From then on, the privacy policy of the place you chose applies.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the result to the app you choose. FileMint does not see what that app does with it.
  • Originals: FileMint never modifies or deletes your original files.

3. What the App Stores on Your Device

  • History: a list of completed operations (file name, tool, output format, size, date and a reference to the saved file), so you can reopen or share results. It never contains copies of your files.
  • Settings: theme, default quality, metadata preference and number of parallel workers.

This information stays on your phone and is used only to show your history and apply your settings.

4. Photo Metadata (EXIF)

Photos can contain metadata such as the capture date, camera model and location. By default FileMint copies this metadata into converted JPEG, PNG and WebP files so they stay organised in your gallery. Turn off Settings → Defaults → "Keep photo metadata" to remove it from new files. Metadata is never read for any other purpose and never leaves your device. PDFs created by FileMint do not include photo metadata.

5. Permissions

  • FileMint requests no Android permissions: no internet, storage, camera, location, contacts, microphone or notification permissions.
  • File access works through Android's file picker, which grants access only to the files and folders you choose.
  • The installed app lists one internal permission, org.tahrik.filemint.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, added automatically by an Android support library. It only protects FileMint's own components and gives no access to your data.

6. Third-Party Services

  • FileMint does not use any third-party services, SDKs that collect data, advertising networks or cloud services.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • History: remove single entries or clear the whole list from the History screen or Settings.
  • Temporary files: clear them in Settings → Storage.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes its history, settings and cache. Files you saved elsewhere are not affected.
  • Because we hold no personal data, we cannot access, correct or delete data on your behalf, and we have nothing to hand over to anyone, including in response to legal requests.

8. Children's Privacy

FileMint does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com.
