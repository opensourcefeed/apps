Decordle
Word game · Android

8 word puzzles, fresh every day

Decordle brings 8 word puzzles together in one free app, each with a new daily puzzle. Open the app and the Today screen shows how many you have solved, with one tap to the next. Want more? Every game also has unlimited or practice play.

  • Version 3.7.1
  • 8 games
  • Daily & unlimited
  • Word of the Day
Get it onGoogle Play
 See the games

The games

8 games, a new puzzle in each every day

Start with a single word, take on ten at once, or try something different with grids, chains and crosswords.

🔤

Guess the words

  • One Word: find the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Works offline
  • Two Words: solve two words with one set of guesses
  • Five Words: five boards, one keyboard
  • Ten Words: the big challenge, ten words at once
🧩

Something different

  • Word Gridle: fill the grid using only the letters you are given
  • Word Plant: find the word letter by letter and grow a plant
  • Word Tail: chain words where each starts with the last letter of the one before
  • Daily Cross: a fresh crossword every day, plus Journey levels
📅

Today, at a glance

  • Today screen: a progress ring for all 8 daily puzzles
  • One tap: continue straight to the next unfinished daily
  • Daily or unlimited: pick the mode for each game in a tap
  • Same puzzle for everyone: compare with friends without spoilers
📖

Learn a word a day

  • Word of the Day: with pronunciation and meaning, never spoiling today's puzzle
  • Meanings after you play: see the definition of every One Word answer
  • Hints: stuck? Unlock a hint that shows your letters and the word's meaning
🔥

Streaks & badges

  • Daily streak: come back every day to keep it going
  • Badges: unlock rewards from 2 days up to a full month
  • Stats & Records: wins, streaks and win rate for each game
🎨

Play your way

  • Dark & high contrast: including a colour-blind friendly palette in One Word
  • Hard mode: revealed hints must be used in later guesses
  • Sounds & vibration: short, gentle effects you can switch off
  • Smooth: quick loading and a retry screen if you go offline

How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Type a guess with the on-screen keyboard. After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you are.

ARight letter, right spot
BIn the word, wrong spot
CNot in the word

Tips for winning

  • Limited attempts: six guesses for one word, more when you solve several at once
  • Open strong: start with vowel-rich words
  • Refine: use every colour clue to narrow down the answer
  • Think across boards: in Two, Five and Ten Words, each guess counts on every board

Privacy & connectivity

Simple, transparent, no sign-up

Minimal data collection

  • Local storage: game progress and settings stay on your device
  • Ad-supported: free to play, funded by Google AdMob ads
  • Anonymous analytics: usage and crash reports via Google Firebase help us fix bugs. No names or emails
  • No account required: start playing immediately
  • Minimal permissions: only essential Android permissions

Read the full Decordle privacy policy for details on data handling, advertising and your rights.

Online and offline play

  • Works offline: One Word, daily and unlimited, with a built-in dictionary
  • Needs internet: the other seven games, word meanings and ads
  • Offline? No problem: games that need a connection are marked, with a retry screen

Built with React Native 0.88, TypeScript and Hermes · MMKV storage · Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics · Android 15 edge-to-edge
