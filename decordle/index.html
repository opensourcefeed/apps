Decordle
Word game · Android

The ultimate word puzzle collection

Decordle brings 10+ Wordle-style games together in one free app. Start with the classic single word, then take on 2, 5 or the legendary 10 words at once. Play unlimited puzzles any time, or come back every day for fresh daily challenges.

  • Version 3.1.0
  • 10+ game modes
  • Daily & unlimited
  • Colour-blind mode

Game modes

From one word to ten at once

Warm up with a classic, then stretch your vocabulary with multi-board challenges.

📝

Classic word puzzles

  • One Word Unlimited: the classic experience with unlimited plays, including a native offline mode
  • One Word Daily: a new word challenge every day
  • Two Words (Dordle): solve two words simultaneously, unlimited play
🚀

Advanced challenges

  • Five Words (Pentordle): master five words at once, daily and unlimited
  • Ten Words (Decordle): the ultimate ten-word challenge, daily and unlimited
  • Word Gridle: a unique grid-based word puzzle, daily and unlimited
  • Word Plant: a creative word-growing puzzle
🎯

Gameplay

  • Difficulty that scales: from 1-word beginner to 10-word expert
  • Hard mode: revealed hints must be used in later guesses
  • Smart validation: real-time word checking against a full dictionary
  • Clear feedback: green, yellow and grey tiles after every guess
📊

Stats & progress

  • Statistics: win rate, streaks and performance metrics
  • Badges: unlock achievements for milestones and daily streaks
  • History: review your games and see yourself improve
🎨

Make it yours

  • Dark mode: a beautiful dark theme
  • High contrast mode: colour-blind friendly orange and blue palette
  • Native or web: choose native or WebView-based gameplay
  • Smooth animations: polished tile flips and transitions
⚡

Performance

  • Hybrid connectivity: native mode works offline; most modes need internet
  • Fast loading: optimised with React Native and the Hermes engine
  • Lightweight: efficient MMKV storage for settings

How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Type a guess with the on-screen keyboard. After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you are.

ARight letter, right spot
BIn the word, wrong spot
CNot in the word

Tips for winning

  • Limited attempts: six guesses for a single word, more for multi-word modes
  • Open strong: start with vowel-rich words
  • Refine: use every colour clue to narrow down the answer
  • Think across boards: in multi-word modes, each guess counts on every board

Privacy & connectivity

Simple, transparent, no sign-up

Minimal data collection

  • Local storage: game progress and settings stay on your device (MMKV)
  • Ad-supported: free to play, funded by Google AdMob ads
  • No account required: start playing immediately
  • Minimal permissions: only essential Android permissions

Read the full Decordle privacy policy for details on data handling, advertising and your rights.

Online and offline play

  • Needs internet: most game modes (WebView-based) and ads
  • Works offline: native One Word Unlimited with a pre-loaded dictionary
  • Daily challenges: need internet to fetch the day's puzzles

Built with React Native 0.77, TypeScript and Hermes · MMKV storage · React Native Sound · react-native-bootsplash
