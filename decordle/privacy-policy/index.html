Decordle Privacy Policy

Effective December 4, 2025 · Last updated November 2, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Decordle, developed by Tahrik Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how our word puzzle game collection handles your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Game Settings: Your preferences for dark mode, high contrast mode, hard mode, and native/WebView mode selection
  • Game Progress: Your gameplay statistics, win/loss records, streak data, and badge achievements
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Android version, device model, and app version for compatibility purposes
  • Usage Data: Anonymous app events such as opening the app, starting or finishing a game, the mode played, the number of guesses, hint use and sharing a result, collected with Google Firebase Analytics
  • Crash Reports: When the app crashes, a report with the error, device model, Android version, app version and the game that was open, collected with Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID for ad personalization (can be reset in Android settings)

1.3 Information Not Collected

We do NOT collect:

  • Your exact location or GPS coordinates
  • Contact lists or phone numbers
  • Photos, files, or other media from your device
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Banking or payment information

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Game Functionality

  • Store your game progress, statistics, and settings locally on your device
  • Track daily streaks and unlock badge achievements
  • Remember your preferences (dark mode, difficulty settings, etc.)
  • Load daily puzzles and unlimited game modes

2.2 Advertising

  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Support the free availability of the app through ad revenue
  • Allow you to opt out of personalized ads via Android settings

2.3 App Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve game design and features
  • Identify and fix bugs or performance issues
  • Understand which game modes are most popular

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

  • MMKV Storage: Game settings and preferences stored locally using fast MMKV library
  • On-Device Only: Your game progress, statistics and streaks stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers
  • No Cloud Sync: Data is not backed up or synced across devices

3.2 Data Retention

  • Game data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • We do not run our own servers for app data. Anonymous analytics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase for a limited period (up to 14 months for analytics events and 90 days for crash reports) and then deleted
  • Uninstalling the app completely removes all local data

3.3 Security Measures

  • Standard Android security protocols protect app data
  • No transmission of sensitive personal information; analytics and crash reports are sent over encrypted connections
  • Regular security updates through app updates

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

Decordle uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • AdMob may use your Advertising ID for personalized ads
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings
  • AdMob may collect device information and usage data

4.2 Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Since version 3.7, Decordle uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find and fix crashes. This data is anonymous: it is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name, email or phone number. It is processed by Google according to the Firebase privacy information.

  • Firebase Analytics: app opens and in-game events listed in section 1.2
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, including the last few in-app events before the crash
  • We use this data only to improve the app. We do not sell it or use it for advertising

4.3 Web Content (WebView Mode)

Seven of the eight games load web-based puzzles from opensourcefeed.org inside the app. When using these games:

  • Internet Required: Web-based game modes require internet connectivity
  • Cookies: Web content may use cookies and browser storage
  • Third-Party Privacy: Web content is subject to opensourcefeed.org's privacy practices

4.4 Dictionary Lookups

  • To show a word's meaning (Word of the Day, hints and after a game), the app sends that single word to the free Dictionary API at dictionaryapi.dev. No other data is sent

4.5 Google Play Services

  • App updates and distribution through Google Play Store
  • In-app review prompts through Google Play, at most once every 90 days
  • Subject to Google Play's Privacy Policy

5. Internet Connectivity

Hybrid Online/Offline App: Decordle requires different levels of internet access depending on the game mode:

5.1 Internet Required For:

  • Web Games: Two Words, Five Words, Ten Words, Word Gridle, Word Plant, Word Tail and Daily Cross load from opensourcefeed.org
  • Word Meanings: Looking up definitions
  • Analytics and Crash Reports: Sent when a connection is available
  • Advertisements: Loading and displaying ads
  • App Updates: Downloading updates from Google Play

5.2 Works Offline:

  • One Word (Daily and Unlimited): Fully playable offline with a built-in word list
  • Settings: All app settings accessible offline
  • Statistics: View your stats and achievements offline

6. Children's Privacy

Decordle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and contains family-friendly content. Parents should supervise children's use of the app, especially regarding advertisements.

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Data Control

  • Reset Statistics: Clear your game data within app settings
  • Delete Data: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Advertising Settings: Manage ad personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads

7.2 Opt-Out Options

  • Disable personalized ads through Android device settings
  • Reset your Advertising ID to prevent ad tracking continuity
  • Choose native mode over WebView mode for reduced internet dependency
  • Analytics and crash reports: there is no in-app switch yet. To have this anonymous data deleted, email us; uninstalling the app stops all collection

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • Email: contact.opensourcefeed@gmail.com
  • Developer: Tahrik Studio
  • App: Decordle (org.tahrik.wordle)

10. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:

  • Google Play Store policies and guidelines
  • Android app privacy requirements
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles for EU users
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) principles for California users

  • ✅ We don't collect personal information
  • ✅ Anonymous usage and crash reports (Google Firebase) help us fix bugs
  • ✅ The app shows ads to stay free
  • ✅ Most games need internet; One Word works offline
  • ✅ You can delete all data by uninstalling the app
  • ✅ You control your ad personalization in Android settings
